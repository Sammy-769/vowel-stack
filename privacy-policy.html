Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vowel Stack (“the Game”) handles information when you play the Game or use its online features. By using Vowel Stack, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Vowel Stack may store information necessary to provide game features and save player progress. This may include:

  • Player name or username
  • Account information used by the Game
  • Game progress and level completion
  • High scores and leaderboard scores
  • Coins, XP, English rank, and unlocked content
  • Power-ups and customization items
  • Daily challenge and reward progress
  • Equipped Cubey appearance
  • Public profile message
  • Other game-related progress and settings

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as financial information, government identification numbers, or precise location information.

2. Cloud Accounts and Game Progress

Players may use online account features to save and synchronize their progress. Cloud data may include scores, coins, XP, level progress, stars, unlocked items, equipped items, daily progress, and other information required to restore and synchronize the player's game state.

3. Leaderboards and Public Profiles

Vowel Stack includes online leaderboard and player profile features. Information that players choose to make public may be visible to other players, including:

  • Player name
  • Leaderboard position and scores
  • English/XP rank
  • Equipped Cubey appearance
  • Public profile message

Players should not include sensitive or personally identifiable information in their player name or public profile message.

When a player chooses to interact with one of these links, they may be redirected to an external website or service operated by a third party.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and may use technologies such as cookies, device information, IP addresses, or other identifiers.

Vowel Stack does not control the privacy practices, content, or availability of third-party websites. Players should review the privacy policies and terms of any external service they visit.

5. Internet Access

Some Vowel Stack features require an internet connection, including cloud accounts, leaderboards, online profiles, and certain promotional or advertising features. Core game functionality may be available without using all online features.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Vowel Stack may be used to:

  • Save and restore game progress
  • Synchronize player accounts
  • Operate leaderboards
  • Display player profiles
  • Provide daily challenges and rewards
  • Maintain unlocked and equipped items
  • Improve game functionality
  • Prevent abuse or incorrect game data
  • Provide online and advertising-related features

Player information is not sold by the developer for monetary payment.

7. Third-Party Services

Vowel Stack may rely on third-party services for features such as cloud storage, online functionality, leaderboards, or advertising. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
